FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Do you work outside Salem?

Yes. We work with businesses anywhere in India over phone, WhatsApp and video calls. If you are in or near Salem, you can also meet us in person at our office.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your business name and you get all logins. If you ever move to someone else, everything goes with you.

How long does a website take?

A standard business website usually takes one to two weeks once we have your content, photos and logo. We can help you write the content too.

When will the billing software be ready?

We are building it now, starting with medical stores, grocery shops and clinics. Join the early list and we will contact you first for a demo.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% on completion for one-time work, and monthly for care plans. We accept UPI and bank transfer, with a proper GST invoice.

We are not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We explain everything in simple language, in Tamil or English, and train you or your staff until you are comfortable.

What if something stops working?

Every project includes 30 days of free fixes. After that, the Valarodigital Care plan covers support, or we help on a per-request basis.
