1. Who we are

Valarodigital is the trade name of Valaro Enterprises, based in Salem, Tamil Nadu, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect on valarodigital.com, why, and what you can do about it. You can reach us at hello@valarodigital.com or +91 79049 31067.

2. Information we collect

  • Free Business Consultation form: your name, mobile number, email address, the service you are interested in, and an optional note. We also record that you ticked the consent box (with the date, time and the exact wording you agreed to) and a scrambled, one-way code made from your internet address, which we use only to stop spam. We do not keep your internet address itself.
  • Messages to us: anything you send on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Purchases: when you buy from us, your name, email address, mobile number, state, pincode and, if you choose to give it, your address. We need these for your GST invoice. Payment is processed by the payment provider. We do not see or store your card or UPI details.
  • Reader account: to read a book online you sign in with your email address and a one-time code that we email to you. There is no password. We keep your purchase record and basic sign-in details, such as the devices and times you signed in, to protect the book from being shared.
  • Technical data: this website does not use analytics or advertising cookies at present. When you sign in to read a book, a small session cookie keeps you signed in. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. If we add analytics later, we will update this page first.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes. For the consultation form, we call, WhatsApp or email you about your request, only because you agreed when you submitted the form.
  • To deliver your purchase, give you online access to the book and email your tax invoice.
  • To answer questions and give support.
  • To improve the website and our services.
  • To meet legal and accounting requirements.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the services we use to run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • the payment provider that processes your payment;
  • Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, which stores your account and purchase record;
  • our email provider, which sends your login code and invoice;
  • Google, where our email runs;
  • WhatsApp, when you contact us there;
  • Cloudflare, whose Turnstile security check on our enquiry form receives your internet address and browser details to tell people from bots; it does not use them for advertising;
  • our website hosting provider; and
  • authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep consultation enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them when you ask us to, or after 12 months if they did not lead to any work. We keep order and invoice records for as long as accounting and legal rules require.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, as far as Indian law allows. Email hello@valarodigital.com from the address you used with us and we will reply.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or storage service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Children

Our website and services are not directed at children under 18. Please do not send us a child's personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
