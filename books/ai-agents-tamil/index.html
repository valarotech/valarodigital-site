About the book

A beginner-friendly guide in Tamil to building and managing AI agents with Claude Code. It is practical and written in an easy, spoken-Tamil style, so you can follow it even if the subject is new to you.

Who it is for

Beginners new to AI tools, working professionals who want to make their daily work easier, and software developers and architects who want to understand how to design agents. No deep technical experience is needed.
